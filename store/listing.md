# Flip Jar, Play Store listing

## App name (30 characters max)
Flip Jar: Merge Puzzle

## Short description (80 characters max)
Drop, merge, and flip the whole jar. One thumb, one daily jar, endless chaos.

## Full description (4000 characters max)
Drop jelly pieces into the jar. Two of the same kind touch, they merge into something bigger. Fill the jar and it overflows.

But Flip Jar has a trick no other merge game has: you can FLIP THE WHOLE JAR. Three times per run, turn the jar upside down and watch the entire pile tumble, collide and chain-merge. Time it right and a losing jar becomes a high score. Time it wrong and... well, that is half the fun.

ONE THUMB, NO TUTORIAL
Drag to aim, release to drop. You will understand it in three seconds and still be improving after a hundred runs.

THE DAILY JAR
Every day, every player in the world gets the exact same sequence of pieces. Three tries, best score counts. Share your result and find out who really plays better.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
Pieces stretch as they fall and wobble like jelly when they land. Every merge pops. Every flip shakes the screen. Haptics on, sound on, and a run becomes something you feel.

FREE PLAY
No daily limit, no pressure, just a jar and a quiet evening.

STREAKS AND THEMES
Play the daily jar on consecutive days to build a streak and unlock new color themes.

WORKS OFFLINE
No account, no internet needed, no ads, no data collection. Small download, runs on modest phones.

Can you reach the Sun?
