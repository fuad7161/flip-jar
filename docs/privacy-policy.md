# Flip Jar Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 6 November 2026**

Flip Jar is a free offline puzzle game published by Fuad (the "developer").

## What the game collects

Flip Jar does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The game has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Your scores, daily attempts, streaks, chosen theme and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**: used for haptic feedback on merges and flips. It can be turned off in the game's settings.
- **Internet**: used only when you tap "Share on WhatsApp", which opens a WhatsApp link with the text of your result. The game never sends data on its own.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds online features such as leaderboards or ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app listing will link to the new version.
